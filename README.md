# TRACE-X 🔍

### AI-Powered Email Threat Detection, GeoLocation & Forensic Intelligence Platform

> **Detect. Trace. Correlate. Investigate.**

TRACE-X is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform designed to detect suspicious and fraudulent emails, analyze their technical structure, trace probable origins, correlate associated infrastructure, and generate forensic intelligence for investigation.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Email remains a major attack vector for:

- Phishing
- Spoofing & impersonation
- Business Email Compromise (BEC)
- Financial fraud
- Credential theft
- Malicious links & attachments

Traditional email security solutions often focus on **filtering and blocking**, while deeper investigation requires analysts to manually correlate email headers, IP addresses, domains, relay paths, geolocation data, and threat intelligence across multiple tools.

### The gap

> **Detecting a malicious email is only the first step.  
> TRACE-X helps investigate where it came from and what infrastructure is connected to it.**
